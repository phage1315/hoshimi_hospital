At the nurses' station, Hiroko is briefing an unfamiliar nurse. The woman neatly puts several handover sheets into a folder, then looks up at Sakaguchi as she hears footsteps.
“Doctor, perfect timing. Let me introduce someone.”
“This is Saeko Hikami. She's just transferred here and will be working in internal medicine.”
“Saeko Hikami. I look forward to working with you.”
She bows slightly, her voice steady. Rather than nervous, she seems to have already prepared everything she needs to say on her first day.
“Have you started the handover already?”
“Yes. Ms. Sugimura is showing me the wards and the duty schedule.”
“The paperwork is all done. We can teach her the rest as she works.”
Sakaguchi nods, then glances at Saeko again.
“Hikami... I heard you had another reason for coming here?”
“That's what my character profile says.”
“...What?”
“Nothing. Please go on.”
Her expression does not change. Sakaguchi looks at Hiroko, who lowers her eyes and turns a page of the duty roster.
“Anyway, get used to the work first. We can talk about your situation later.”
“Please don't say later.”
This time, her answer comes quickly.
“What's wrong?”
“That's how I waited last time, too.”
“Last time?”
“They said I had an ulterior motive, a mysterious background, and probably a story coming later.”
“Then the series got axed, the setup never paid off, and all I could do was go play mahjong.”
“...”
“So, could you put my story on the schedule first this time?”
Hiroko pulls out a sheet of paper and hands it to her.
“I've put you on tomorrow's early shift first.”
Saeko takes the roster and studies it for a while, then checks the name on it once more.
“...At least I'm working as a nurse this time.”
