The nurses' station is busier this morning than Sakaguchi expected. Ren Nanase is checking the handover records when he hands her a surgery request awaiting confirmation.
“Nanase, isn't there still an opening in the operating room tomorrow morning? I wanted to ask whether we could move this operation up.”
“Let me check... Yes, there is time available in the operating room.”
“Then we can schedule it?”
“Not yet. That schedule only covers the operating rooms, not the nursing staff.”
She places the request on the nursing liaison folder.
“I'll have to ask the nursing department about this first.”
“...Is it that strict?”
Ren nods and takes the request toward the office area inside the nurses' station.
Sakaguchi remains beside the desk at the nurses' station. Ren Nanase has left the frame with the request. The scene stays in the same location. After a brief silence, a cool female voice comes from behind him.
“Would you like me to explain why?”
Sakaguchi turns around.
The woman before him is tall and slender, with black hair. Her clothes are neat and practical, and the badge on her chest identifies her nursing management role. She carries two different staffing schedules and the request from a moment ago. Her face shows no particular displeasure, nor any inclination to make small talk.
“Chizuru Ishigami. Nursing department.”
“Ryuji Sakaguchi.”
“I know.”
Ishigami places the two schedules separately on the desk and sets the surgery request to one side.
“This is the operating room schedule.”
She points to the other sheet.
“This is the nursing staff allocation schedule.”
“You were looking at the first one.”
“...I haven't checked the second one, that's true.”
“Then don't say it can already be scheduled.”
Sakaguchi looks down more closely. The available operating room slots do not neatly line up with the nurses' handover times.
“So all that's free here is the room.”
“Correct.”
She stacks the schedules neatly again, her tone as level as before.
“Nurses don't automatically materialize in operating rooms.”
For a moment, Sakaguchi has no reply.
Ishigami does not hurry him or take the opportunity to lecture him. She simply points out the nursing handover on the schedule.
“During this period tomorrow morning, the wards need to complete handover. The afternoon circulating staff already have assignments, too.”
“What if we made a last-minute adjustment?”
“We can discuss it. First establish whose assignment changes and who takes over their original duties.”
“I only wanted the patient to wait one day less.”
“I accept that reason.”
Sakaguchi raises his head a little. But she has not agreed to the request.
“But saving the patient a day's wait cannot mean giving a nurse an extra shift.”
“I didn't mean to make them work overtime.”
“Intentions don't turn themselves into staffing schedules.”
Ishigami closes the schedules and looks at Sakaguchi.
“I'm not here to stop you from operating.”
“Doctors decide when an operation should be done. The nursing department confirms whether there are people who can safely take on the work.”
“Understood. How should I submit the request, then?”
Ishigami seems a little more satisfied with this question than with his earlier explanation.
“Send the nursing department the estimated duration, the scrub and circulating nurses required, and the postoperative handover requirements in advance.”
“If we can adjust the schedule, I'll agree. If we can't, I'll tell you why.”
“And if there really isn't a way to fit it in?”
“We find the next suitable time together.”
She pauses briefly.
“This is a hospital. Doctors, nurses, and patients all need to be able to count on arrangements that work for them.”
Sakaguchi picks up the request again and turns to the nursing coordination section he had left blank.
“Then I'll fill this in properly first. I won't promise the patient a specific time before the nursing department confirms it.”
“That's right.”
“From now on, I'll notify the nursing department first when adding an operation at short notice.”
“No need to wait until next time. Finish this one first.”
Sakaguchi cannot help smiling a little. Ishigami's expression does not change.
“Head Nurse Ishigami, I suspect I'll be troubling you quite often from now on.”
“If you fill in the information clearly, it isn't trouble.”
She picks up the nursing allocation schedule, preparing to leave.
“One more thing.”
“Yes?”
“When the wards are busy, please don't discuss who is unwilling to work overtime in front of patients.”
“The nursing department will handle the coordination. All the patient needs to know is whether the hospital has made proper arrangements for her.”
“I'll remember that.”
Ishigami nods slightly and leaves the nurses' station with the schedules. She does not smile or add any polite pleasantries.
Sakaguchi looks at the unfinished request in his hand, picks up a pen, and fills in the estimated duration, staffing needs, and handover arrangements in the nursing coordination section.
