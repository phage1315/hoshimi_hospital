[Nurses' Station / Morning]
The nurses' station is already busy this morning. Chizuru Ishigami is checking a stack of internship registration papers. An unfamiliar young woman stands in front of the desk.
The girl wears a neat nurse's uniform, but does not yet have a permanent staff name badge. Holding a notebook filled with sticky notes, she stands very straight, smiling but a little nervous.
Ishigami takes a neatly folded sheet of paper from the documents.
“Kariya, what is this?”
“Oh, that's some information I put together myself.”
“Staff nurses' pay, benefits, and comparisons with other hospitals...?”
“Yes! I spent ages researching it. Since I'm going to be an intern, I thought I should find out whether I might be able to stay afterward.”
Ishigami looks at her.
“I have no objection to your researching pay and benefits. But this doesn't belong among the ward handover documents.”
“I-I'm sorry! I put it in the wrong place when I was sorting everything!”
She quickly takes the sheet back and slips it into her notebook. The handwriting and tables on it are clearly very neat.
Just then, Ryuji Sakaguchi enters the nurses' station.
“Dr. Sakaguchi, perfect timing. Let me introduce an intern who is starting today.”
The girl immediately turns toward Sakaguchi and bows carefully.
“It's nice to meet you! I'm Megumi Kariya, and I'm starting my internship here today. I look forward to working with you!”
“Hello, Kariya. You're certainly full of energy for your first day.”
“Of course! I've really been looking forward to this internship.”
“The internship begins on June first and lasts six months. The final assessment will be on December first. The nursing department will arrange your duties. Start by learning the daily routines in each department.”
“Yes! I've already read all the instructions you sent me.”
Hiroko Sugimura, who is sorting patient records nearby, looks up at her with a smile.
“I hear you're keen to work at our hospital after you graduate?”
“I'd like to stay after graduation.”
“You've already decided?”
“Yes. I've compared the offers, and the pay and benefits here are the best.”
She pauses, then hastily adds something.
“...I mean, there's so much I can learn here!”
“There's nothing wrong with what you said the first time.”
“Huh?”
“It's perfectly normal to want to earn money through your own work.”
Megumi visibly relaxes.
“That's a relief. I was worried that saying that on my first day would give everyone a bad impression of me.”
“My family needs the money, and I want to become independent sooner, too. If I'm going to study hard and work hard during my internship, of course I want a well-paying job afterward.”
“Sounds like you've really done your homework.”
“Yes! I'm confident about that part.”
“Then let's see how much homework you've done on nursing.”
“...Yes!”
The phone at the nurses' station rings. Hiroko is busy checking the handover documents. Megumi instinctively reaches for it, then stops and looks at Ishigami.
“May I answer it?”
Ishigami nods.
“Listen carefully to what the caller needs first. If you're unsure about something, leave the answer to the person responsible.”
Megumi picks up the phone. Her slightly embarrassed expression immediately turns serious.
“Hello, you've reached the nurses' station. This is Kariya speaking.”
“...All right. Take your time. I'll write it down.”
She opens her notebook and takes notes on a blank page, nodding gently as she listens.
“You'd like to confirm the time of your preoperative tests, is that right?”
“I'm not able to answer that myself yet. May I check with the nurse in charge and have her get back to you?”
The person on the other end says a little more.
“Yes, I understand you're worried. I'll make sure your question is passed on clearly. Please hold.”
She puts down the phone and hands her notes to Hiroko.
“Ms. Sugimura, this patient wants to confirm her preoperative test schedule. I've written down her name, ward, and question, but I don't know the exact time, so I couldn't just give her an answer.”
Hiroko takes the paper and glances over it.
“Thank you. These are very clear notes. I'll contact the nurse responsible.”
“Great! I'll take notes like this next time, too.”
“One more thing, Kariya.”
“Yes?”
“It's good that you took the initiative to check what you didn't know on the phone. But you don't need to rush to do everyone's work on your very first day.”
“Oh... am I getting ahead of myself?”
“A little.”
“Understood. I'll be more careful.”
She quickly adds a line to her notebook.
Hiroko sets a rotation schedule in front of her.
“During your internship, you'll experience the work of different departments. You'll gradually learn the nursing routines on the wards, in outpatients, and in the operating room.”
“The wards, outpatients...”
At the words “operating room,” her pen suddenly stops.
“...I have to go to the operating room, too.”
“What's wrong? Don't you like operating rooms?”
“It's not that I don't like them. It's just... I've never actually stood beside someone having an operation.”
“I can look at all the pictures at school. But when I see it for real... I don't know how I'll react.”
She tries to keep smiling, but her fingers unconsciously tighten around the edge of her notebook.
“If I don't do well then, will it affect my internship assessment?”
“Your assessment is based on how you learn and work over these six months. It won't be decided by your first attempt alone.”
“If you really feel unwell, tell a senior nurse right away. Pushing yourself until you can't even look after yourself won't help the patient.”
“...All right. I'll remember that.”
She looks down, writes another line, then raises her head again.
“Then I'll keep studying ahead! I spent ages memorizing the operating room preparation procedure yesterday. I can't let that go to waste.”
“Being prepared is good. But once you're actually there, you also need to judge how you're coping.”
“Yes, Dr. Sakaguchi.”
Just then, Ange Tonegawa enters the nurses' station carrying a neatly sorted stack of patient records.
“Ms. Ishigami, the ward records are all sorted. Is there anything else I can help with?”
“Good timing. Ange, show Kariya, who is starting today, the routes around the wards and the nurses' station.”
Ange turns to Megumi with a friendly smile.
“Hello, I'm Ange Tonegawa. If there's anything you're unsure about, you can ask me.”
“Hi! I'm Megumi Kariya. You look about my age.”
“Yes, I'm eighteen.”
“Really? I'm eighteen, too!”
She suddenly notices Ange's permanent nurse's name badge.
“Oh, you're already a staff nurse?”
“Yes, but I still have plenty to learn.”
“I see...”
She looks at Ange's badge for a moment, then smiles.
“Then I'll have to work hard to catch up with you. In six months, I want to wear a badge like that, too.”
“Then let's both do our best. Come with me and learn your way around the wards first.”
“Okay!”
Megumi gathers her notebook and follows Ange toward the nurses' station exit. After a couple of steps, she turns back and bows earnestly to Sakaguchi, Hiroko, and Ishigami.
“I'm really looking forward to working with all of you, starting today!”
“Welcome to Hoshimi Hospital.”
“There's no need to rush. Learn one thing at a time.”
“I know. But... I've already done the math.”
“The math for what?”
“If I can stay here, life will be a lot easier financially for both me and my family. I'm going to work hard for these six months to make that happen.”
She speaks with complete sincerity, then returns to her cheerful smile.
“Well then, I'd better get to work!”
Ange waits for her by the door. The two leave the nurses' station side by side.
Once outside, Megumi quietly glances through the rotation schedule.
“Ange... do you see blood very often in the operating room?”
“Yes. Of course you'll see blood in an operating room.”
“...I thought so.”
She falls silent for a moment, then closes her notebook.
“It's all right. I'll focus on learning what I can today!”
She quickens her steps to catch up with Ange. Back in the nurses' station, the phone rings again amid the day's work.
