As Sakaguchi passes the nurses' station, he sees Hiroko Sugimura checking handover documents with a young nurse wearing black-framed glasses. The girl's voice is quiet, but she explains every item clearly.
“The patient in bed seven has completed all her preoperative tests. She says she doesn't want to cancel the operation. She just wants someone to tell her when they'll come to collect her.”
“Right. Then confirm the expected transfer time, too. Don't just write ‘explained.’”
“Yes, I've already asked the operating room. Once she heard the time, she finally put down that information leaflet.”
“Good. You remembered what the patient was actually worried about. That's more important than filling every box on the form.”
“Thank you. I still have a long way to go, though...”
“I've always looked up to you, Hiroko.”
“I want to be a nurse like you, someone who helps patients feel at ease.”
“Then start with today's handover. You're already doing well.”
Sakaguchi approaches the counter. Hiroko looks up, and Satsuki turns with her. The instant she sees who it is, the hand reaching for her glasses freezes in midair.
“Good morning, Hiroko. Do I confirm this transfer arrangement with you?”
“Yes, Doctor. Perfect timing—let me introduce you. This is Satsuki Orikawa. She's recently started working alongside me to learn the routines.”
“Satsuki, this is Dr. Ryuji Sakaguchi from surgery.”
“S-Sakaguchi... D-Doctor...!”
“Hello, Orikawa. I heard your handover just now. Your notes were very clearly organized.”
“Th-thank you! N-no, I mean, I'm still... um...”
Satsuki's voice grows quieter and quieter. Instinctively, she moves half a step behind Hiroko, her fingers still gripping the handover folder tightly.
“Satsuki, put the file down for a moment. The doctor is only saying hello.”
“I-I'm sorry! I know...”
“It's all right. If you have any questions about transfers or handovers, just ask me directly.”
“A-ask you... directly?”
“Of course. You don't have to pass everything through Hiroko.”
“...Yes!”
Only after making sure Sakaguchi is no longer looking her way does Satsuki slowly loosen her grip on the folder and bow her head to apologize to Hiroko.
“Hiroko... was I terribly rude just now?”
“You greeted the doctor, and you didn't hold up the handover. Your nerves were just a little too obvious.”
“Ugh... I know. I had it all ready in my head, but when I saw a male doctor coming toward me, I forgot everything.”
“You didn't forget what to say when you were talking about the patient.”
“Th-that's work. The patient is waiting. Of course I have to explain things clearly...”
As she says this, she looks down and leafs through the duty roster in her hands. Her fingertip pauses between the surgery and gynecology columns.
“I really did want to be a surgical nurse like you, Hiroko...”
“But I applied to gynecology after all.”
“Because there are fewer male patients?”
“...Because there aren't any male patients.”
Hiroko looks at her, then at the ward information in her hand, as though she has finally realized they have been talking about different things from the start.
“Satsuki, didn't you know?”
“This hospital only admits female patients.”
“...Huh?”
Satsuki abruptly raises her head, and her black-framed glasses slide a little down her nose. Holding the frames, she stares at Hiroko, clearly reconsidering what her department application actually meant.
“Huh?????”
