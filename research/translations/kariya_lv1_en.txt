001|At eight forty-five in the morning, sunlight through the blinds cast quiet bars of light across the pale ward floor.
002|Twenty-two-year-old Makoto Konno lay rigid in the middle of the hospital bed, both hands clutching the edge of the thin cotton blanket against her chest, barely daring to breathe.
003|Hiroko Sugimura clipped the completed preoperative checklist back onto her board and gently tucked the blanket, which had slipped a little, around Makoto's shoulders. Her voice was warm and brisk:
004|“Your temperature, blood pressure, and this morning's bowel preparation are all checked. I've checked the skin preparation on your lower right abdomen, too. Miss Konno, all your ward preparations are complete.”
005|Makoto's throat was dry beneath the blanket. Her bare toes curled awkwardly against the sheet, and her muffled voice squeezed out above the blanket's edge:
006|“...Um, Nurse Sugimura. What I'm having today... really is just an ordinary appendectomy, right?”
007|Hiroko smiled and nodded:
008|“Yes. An elective open appendectomy, with Dr. Sakaguchi operating. It's a well-established procedure with a low risk, so try not to worry too much.”
009|Looking close to tears, Makoto pulled the blanket a little higher:
010|“If it's only a simple little operation... why couldn't I eat or drink from last night, and why did I need an enema first thing this morning, and all that shaving below my tummy, and then even my hospital gown and underwear had to come off...?!”
011|Even with the blanket wrapped securely around her, the sensation of lying completely bare against the sheet beneath it was vivid enough to make her scalp prickle.
012|Hiroko put away the bedside tray without a change of expression and explained in Hoshimi Hospital's characteristically gentle but unquestionable professional tone:
013|“Even for a routine appendectomy, we follow the operating room's sterility and transfer procedures on the fourth floor. Taking everything off and covering you with a blanket here means you won't have to change again beside the operating table. It also makes preparing and draping the area cleaner and safer.”
014|She gave the young woman in the bed a reassuring smile:
015|“I'll go upstairs to prepare the instruments. Our trainee nurse Kariya will be here with the stretcher shortly to take you up. Stay covered and rest just as you are—we'll see each other in a few minutes.”
016|The ward door closed softly, and the footsteps faded away.
017|The room fell completely quiet.
018|Usually a whirlwind around friends and coworkers, incapable of sitting still, Makoto now lay utterly motionless on the bed like a salted fish already laid out on a plate.
019|An occasional whiff of disinfectant drifted through the window. Every distant rumble of trolley wheels on the corridor tiles made her heart leap. At any moment, she felt, the door would open and she would be wheeled away, bare beneath one thin blanket, to be cut open under the operating lights.
020|One minute. Two minutes.
021|After the shame and fear had gone around her head a dozen times, stretched to breaking point, Makoto's restless eyes slowly lost their focus.
022|She stared blankly at the pale ceiling tiles. Her fingers loosened a little on the blanket, and her whole body radiated a numb resignation, as if she had seen through all worldly concerns and decided her life was already over.
023|“...Whatever. The consent form's signed, and my clothes are gone... If you're going to wheel me away, just get it over with. The sooner I die, the sooner I'm reborn...”
024|Knock, knock.
025|Two crisp, polite knocks came at the ward door.
026|Then the door opened, and the rubber wheels of a stainless-steel transport stretcher rolled lightly inside. A young, lively voice followed:
027|“Excuse me! Miss Makoto Konno in bed 302, right? Sorry to keep you waiting. I'm Kariya, the trainee nurse taking you to the operating room on the fourth floor today—”
028|Hearing the movement, Makoto turned her vacant gaze from the ceiling toward the door, looking as though her soul had left her body.
029|The girl who stopped the stretcher beside her bed looked about eighteen. In a freshly laundered, pale trainee nurse's uniform, she held a transfer checklist and carefully checked the bed number and name.
030|Makoto's blank gaze settled on the smiling face the girl raised toward her.
031|One second.
032|“...Huh?”
033|Why did that face look... as though she had only just seen it somewhere?
034|The bustling shopping street was brightly lit in the evening. Neat rows of cleaned red pork and ribs lined the refrigerated glass display case.
035|Wearing a dark shop apron behind the counter, Megumi Kariya deftly placed a portion of pork ribs on the electronic scale, glanced at the weight, and tapped the register with a bright voice:
036|“Three hundred and sixty grams of pork ribs, just in time for our evening special! I'll round it down to four hundred and eighty yen. I've wrapped it in two layers of leakproof paper—straight into the pot when you get home! Five hundred yen received, and here's your twenty-yen change. There you go! Thank you, come again!”
037|At the very moment that four-hundred-and-eighty-yen pack of pork ribs flashed through Makoto's mind, Megumi, standing by the stretcher, recognized the young woman in the surgical cap on the bed.
038|The unsuspecting trainee's eyes lit up, and she gave exactly the same brilliant smile she wore when greeting a returning customer at the shop:
039|“Oh! You're the customer who bought pork ribs yesterday evening! What a coincidence!”
040|The ward fell deathly silent for two seconds.
041|Makoto said nothing.
042|She blinked stiffly, her eyes first fixing on Megumi's smiling face;
043|then she slowly lowered her head to look at her own body beneath the thin blanket, stripped completely bare, with even the fine hair below her abdomen freshly shaved away;
044|finally, little by little, she turned toward the metal stretcher beside the bed, spread with a white sheet and waiting to carry her into the operating room.
045|Yesterday evening, she had stood outside a glass counter watching this girl wrap up pieces of cleaned pork and hand them over;
046|this morning, she herself lay stripped and cleaned beneath a white cloth, while the very same meat-shop girl, now dressed in white, pushed a trolley over with a beaming smile to take her inside—
047|Whoosh!
048|A second earlier, Makoto had been lifeless against her pillow, resigned to her fate. Now she shot upright as though struck by high voltage, hugging the blanket tightly over herself. Her pupils shook, and she let out a cracking scream that could have blown the roof off the corridor:
049|“AAAAAAH!! You—you're the girl who was selling meat yesterday!!!”
050|“I don't want a pig butcher operating on me!!!”
051|Megumi had been about to reach for the stretcher brake. Instead she froze beside the bed as though under a spell, both hands still on the metal handle, her customer-service smile stiffening inch by inch:
052|“...Huh?”
053|The scream left Megumi's ears ringing. It took her a full two seconds to recover, and her first instinct was, absurdly, to defend her reputation at her part-time job:
054|“W-wait, Miss Konno! Yes, that was me behind the counter at Marufuku Meats yesterday. I work there after my hospital placement every day—w-was something wrong with the pork ribs I sold you?! I picked the piece with the most even balance of lean and fat!”
055|Makoto huddled by the headboard rail, clinging to her blanket. Far from calming down, she went even paler, her voice trembling uncontrollably:
056|“Who's talking about how fatty the ribs were?! Yesterday you were wrapping pork for me across a counter, and today you're dressed like that, coming to fetch me on a trolley?! First thing this morning they gave me an enema, shaved me, and even took away my last pair of underwear, so I'm lying here completely bare—how is that any different from the meat on your shop's trays waiting to be cut up?!”
057|Megumi's cheeks flushed with panic. She nearly dropped the transfer board and waved both hands desperately as she tried to explain:
058|“N-no, that's not it! I've never slaughtered a pig!! I only work at the front counter, weighing things, wrapping them, taking payments and giving change, for eight hundred and fifty yen an hour! I've never even touched the butcher's chopping board or boning knife! Besides, when the pork arrives in the morning, it's already been slaughtered and split in half—”
059|Makoto sucked in a sharp breath and wrapped the blanket even tighter:
060|“S-split in half?! You even know all about how it's delivered! Once you've pushed me inside, are you going to try a couple of cuts yourself—”
061|Megumi was almost crying with frustration:
062|“That's why I'm telling you I can't cut anything!! I only started my placement at Hoshimi in June! I'm not even qualified to stand at the operating room's instrument table yet, let alone operate on anyone!!”
063|“If you can't even operate, why are you bringing a trolley to fetch me?!”
064|“Because I'm here to push the trolley—!”
065|The two voices, on entirely different wavelengths, collided in midair. For a second, the whole ward seemed to stall.
066|Megumi stopped talking, breathless, and looked down at Makoto in the corner of the bed. This young woman, only a few years older than herself, was gripping the blanket so tightly her knuckles had gone white. Her exposed shoulders trembled with real fear, and tears had even gathered in the corners of her eyes.
067|She wasn't making trouble for no reason, or looking down on anyone's part-time job.
068|She was just an ordinary patient lying bare in an unfamiliar hospital bed, about to be wheeled into an operating room and cut open. And Megumi had been so busy insisting she'd never slaughtered a pig, talking about ribs, boning knives and bodies split in half, that she'd poured fuel on the woman's worst nightmare.
069|The moment she realized that, Megumi snapped her mouth shut.
070|She took a deep breath, let go of the stretcher, and stepped half a pace back to give the patient some space. Then she leaned down slightly until her eyes were level with Makoto's and spoke clearly and firmly, at half her previous speed:
071|“...I'm sorry, Miss Konno. I was so anxious to explain my part-time job that I got everything muddled and frightened you.”
072|Makoto paused, staring at her with the blanket still clutched in her hands.
073|“I really am a newly arrived trainee nurse here. But I've been given just one job today: to push this stretcher and take you safely from this ward to the fourth-floor operating room entrance.”
074|“I won't take the surgeon's position, and I won't touch you with a scalpel. The person actually performing your operation today is one of our excellent, fully qualified surgeons.”
075|Having said that, Megumi didn't immediately reach for the bed rail or hurry her onto the stretcher. She simply stood quietly half a pace away, giving the patient time to get her breathing under control.
076|Just as the tension in the ward visibly began to ease, a steady male voice came from the open doorway:
077|“I'd barely stepped out of the elevator when I heard someone in here shouting about slaughtering pigs.”
078|Makoto and Megumi both turned toward the door.
079|Takashi Sakaguchi stood there in dark scrubs with a white coat loosely over them. His gaze passed over Megumi, standing straight beside the bed, and settled on Makoto's face above her tightly held blanket. His voice was concise, without a trace of unnecessary lecturing:
080|“Don't worry, Miss Konno. I'm the surgeon performing your open appendectomy this morning. She isn't.”
081|He glanced at the visibly relieved Megumi beside him and added, perfectly straight-faced:
082|“Kariya is only taking you safely to the fourth-floor operating room entrance. And as far as I know, Hoshimi charges for appendectomies by the operation, not by the gram.”
083|“Pff—”
084|Megumi almost lost control of her expression. She quickly lowered her head and pressed her lips together.
085|Makoto blinked twice. She looked from the calm, reassuring surgeon at the door to the earnest, empty-handed eighteen-year-old trainee beside the bed, and her mind, overheated with preoperative embarrassment, finally clicked back into reality.
086|Had she really just sat up clutching a blanket and shouted “I don't want a pig butcher operating on me” at a younger trainee who worked a meat-shop register after hours and diligently pushed hospital stretchers during the day?!
087|Blood rushed to her head in an instant, turning Makoto scarlet from the base of her neck to the tips of her ears.
088|The thought of lying on an operating table in a few minutes to have her appendix removed still made her heart flutter. But the absurd terror of being wheeled away like a carcass for carving had vanished, leaving only an embarrassment so crushing she wished the floor would swallow her.
089|Makoto scrambled back down against her pillow and yanked the white blanket up over everything below her nose. Only her flushed, darting eyes remained visible as she mumbled in a mosquito-small voice:
090|“...S-sorry... I was so nervous lying here that my brain just went haywire... I know you're not the one operating on me now...”
091|Seeing how badly the patient wanted to bury herself in the mattress, Sakaguchi didn't linger at her bedside. He considerately tilted his head toward Megumi:
092|“There's no rush over one minute. Rest and catch your breath, Miss Konno. Kariya and I will take the stretcher into the corridor and turn it around. We'll be back for you in half a minute.”
093|There were two quick nods beneath the blanket:
094|“...Okay. Thank you...”
095|Megumi quickly answered, “Yes!” and followed Sakaguchi out with the empty stretcher, gently leaving the ward door ajar.
096|As soon as she was out of the patient's sight, the trainee's carefully straightened shoulders sagged. Hugging the transfer checklist to her chest, she looked up at Sakaguchi with a miserable expression and asked cautiously in a low voice:
097|“Whew... I nearly died of fright back there... Um, Dr. Sakaguchi, making that much of a commotion in a ward—is that the sort of thing that goes on my internship assessment in December?”
098|Sakaguchi stopped and looked at her:
099|“The first thing you worry about once we're outside is your assessment?”
100|The tips of Megumi's ears reddened, but she began doing the practical sums quite frankly, in a quiet voice:
101|“Of course I worry! I worked so hard to get this six-month placement because I calculated that Hoshimi's permanent nurses have the best salary and allowances in the district. If I get rejected before I've even learned the job, for 'frightening a surgical patient by selling meat on the side,' how am I supposed to help my family and save anything on meat-shop shifts at eight hundred and fifty yen an hour...?”
102|Sakaguchi glanced at the checklist clutched in her hands and made a calm joke:
103|“Relax. I've seen Chief Nurse Ishigami's assessment form. There isn't a deduction for 'being mistaken for a pig butcher by an overly nervous patient.'”
104|Megumi blinked, then let out a quiet little laugh:
105|“R-really? That's a relief... She was shouting so loudly, I thought the whole floor was going to come and inspect my work!”
106|“Besides, you steadied things yourself in the end instead of panicking along with her. That's enough for a passing mark.”
107|Meeting her anxious gaze, Sakaguchi gave her a short, practical assessment:
108|“You stumbled over a few things at first. But then you caught yourself, stepped back, and explained your duties to the patient. That part was quite well done—much better than just memorizing the transfer manual.”
109|Megumi froze, not expecting Sakaguchi to have heard the latter half from outside. Her cheeks warmed, but her eyes suddenly brightened:
110|“You heard all that, Dr. Sakaguchi...? Hehe. It was only when I saw Miss Konno shaking so badly that I realized I shouldn't keep talking about the meat shop.”
111|Sakaguchi shook his head and spoke gently:
112|“There's nothing shameful about earning an honest living at a counter after work. Why would the hospital deduct points for that? And you didn't argue with the patient or panic and abandon her. You handled the ending steadily.”
113|Megumi's tense shoulders relaxed completely, and she nodded hard:
114|“Right! As long as it doesn't hurt my year-end retention assessment, I don't mind being misunderstood a little!”
115|Megumi patted her cheeks, her spirits restored, and wheeled the turned-around stretcher back into the ward after Sakaguchi.
116|This time, everything went flawlessly. She secured the stretcher's foot brake, carefully raised the outer edge of the blanket to shield Makoto from view, and steadily helped the crimson-faced patient move across and lie down. She tucked the blanket snugly around her, then clicked both safety rails into place.
117|The stretcher glided out of the ward, along the quiet corridor, and up to the fourth floor.
118|Makoto was still too embarrassed to look up at Megumi pushing behind her. Holding the blanket over the lower half of her face, she stared at the corridor lights passing overhead and muttered in a tiny voice:
119|“Ugh... I've used up a lifetime's worth of embarrassment this morning... If only I could jump straight back to yesterday evening...”
120|The stretcher soon stopped at the clean transfer entrance to the fourth-floor operating department.
121|Already in scrubs and a surgical cap, Hiroko opened the door to meet them. She expertly checked the wristband and took hold of the front of the stretcher:
122|“Thanks for bringing her up, Megumi. I'll take Miss Konno into the operating room from here. Everything's ready inside.”
123|Sakaguchi nodded too:
124|“See you in the operating room shortly, Miss Konno. I'll go scrub.”
125|Watching the patient pass smoothly into her senior's care without a single mishap, Megumi finally let go of the anxiety she'd carried all morning.
126|But in the instant she let out a long breath and her strained nerves relaxed, the customer-service muscle memory she'd drilled in during three hours at the meat-shop counter last night slipped onto her tongue without warning.
127|Megumi put her feet together and bowed neatly to Makoto on the stretcher. A brilliant shop-assistant smile spread across her face as she blurted out, brightly and clearly:
128|“Good luck with your appendix operation, Miss Konno! Once you're better and out of hospital, please come back to our shop—oh, no!!”
129|Megumi clapped a hand over her mouth.
130|The next second, the white blanket on the stretcher gave a violent shudder, and Makoto's mortified scream echoed outside the fourth-floor operating room once again:
131|“Stop bringing up the meat shop—!!”
