Miyuki's eyes immediately curved with satisfaction. She turned, walked to the wall, and switched off the surgical lights with a click.

**[149]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Then it's a promise, my surgeon. Let's go. If we don't leave soon, I really will sleep in and be late tomorrow morning~"
She naturally linked her arm with Ryuji's and walked lightly out of the operating room, leaving it silent once more.

## Part C — The Capable Do More

**[150]**
The nurses' station in the ward was as busy as ever during the day.

**[151]**
Ryuji approached with a chart of orders he had just signed and saw Chizuru Ishigami set a thick stack of rotation assessment forms in front of Miyuki.

**[152]**
Chizuru Ishigami:
"Asakura, you'll be responsible for teaching the two young nurses transferring here next week how to prepare patients in the ward and count instruments."

**[153]**
Miyuki immediately put on a flawless, sweet professional smile.

**[154]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Of course, Nurse Manager Ishigami. Leave it to me."

**[155]**
Chizuru gave a small nod and walked briskly away from the nurses' station.

**[156]**
Almost the very second Chizuru disappeared around the corridor corner, Miyuki's sweet smile collapsed. With a long sigh, she slumped bonelessly over the station counter.

**[157]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Sigh... Somebody save me..."

**[158]**
Ryuji stepped up to the counter and tapped it, amused.

**[159]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"You sounded pretty willing just now."

**[160]**
Miyuki looked up and rolled her eyes at him, jabbing her ballpoint pen into the thick stack of training forms as she complained.

**[161]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"How could I say no in front of Nurse Manager Ishigami? Honestly, I came to Hoshimi Hospital to be an easygoing junior nurse because I was sick of that old semi-management work—supervising people, making schedules, and training new staff..."

**[162]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"And look what happened. I barely managed to pretend I had no experience for a few days, and now whenever Nurse Manager Ishigami has the thankless job of training newcomers, she dumps it on me."

**[163]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"That's what you get for being unable to hide how skilled you are. The capable do more."

**[164]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"You're teasing me too!"

**[165]**
Resting her chin on her hand, she lowered her voice to complain to Ryuji.
"You have no idea. Some of the new nurses are so clumsy they can tie even the simplest sterile pack into an impossible knot. It makes me so angry I want to strap them to the operating table and make an incision so they'll learn their lesson."

**[166]**
Ryuji stopped short and looked at her, momentarily at a loss for words.

**[167]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"............"

**[168]**
Seeing Ryuji's frozen expression, Miyuki stared for half a second, then burst out laughing. She reached across the counter and lightly patted the back of his hand.
"I'm joking! Dr. Sakaguchi, what kind of face is that? Don't tell me you actually believed me?"

**[169]**
Ryuji looked at her lovely eyes, curved like crescents yet always keeping him guessing how much mischief lay behind them, and sighed helplessly.
"I think you really would do it."
