## Part A — Chie's Major Surgery Without Anesthesia

**[001]**
At exactly nine in the morning, the surgical lights in Operating Room One were already glowing. Deep retractors, long-handled tissue scissors, and a complete set of hemostats for a major open abdominal operation stood in a row on the stainless-steel instrument table—twice as many as for an ordinary appendectomy.

**[002]**
Once the transport stretcher had been brought alongside the operating table, the young nurse gently lifted the last thin transfer sheet covering the patient.

**[003]**
Sagamiono Chie:
"Ah—! W-wait! Why do you have to take even this sheet away in the operating room?!"

**[004]**
Chie Sagamiono had already completed her preoperative preparation in the ward and was now completely naked. The moment she felt the operating room's cool air and the leather table beneath her, she flushed from her cheeks to the tips of her toes like a boiled shrimp.

**[005]**
Instinctively, she tried to close her legs and cover her chest and freshly shaved lower abdomen with her arms, but the slight foreign sensation of the catheter tugging against her made her afraid to move.

**[006]**
Standing beside the operating table, Miyuki Asakura smiled with her eyes. She expertly guided Chie's arms onto the armboards on either side while reassuring her in a gentle voice edged with a little teasing.

**[007]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Miss Sagamiono, didn't I explain Hoshimi Hospital's rules in the ward? To maintain sterile conditions for major surgery, patients are transferred without clothes after skin and cleaning preparation in the ward. Once you reach the operating room, you go straight onto the table. We can't keep the sheet that came from the ward over you."

**[008]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"I-I know what you said... but this is so embarrassing! All of you are fully dressed, and I'm the only one lying here naked under the surgical lights!"

**[009]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi walked to the side of the operating table with the chart and looked down to check the final preoperative record.

**[010]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Miss Chie Sagamiono, today's operation is an open extensive intestinal adhesiolysis combined with removal of a benign retroperitoneal cyst. Because the lesion is deep and the adhesions are widespread, the operation itself is expected to take more than four hours. Including preparation and observation after closure, the whole process should take about five hours."

**[011]**
At the words "four or five hours," Chie's fidgeting legs suddenly went rigid.

**[012]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"F-four or five hours?! You're going to cut open my abdomen and work inside it for four or five hours, starting at nine this morning?!"

**[013]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Yes. And according to the request you personally checked on yesterday's consent form and emphasized several times, this major operation will be done entirely without anesthesia. You can still change your mind. Are you certain you don't want general or epidural anesthesia?"

**[014]**
Chie's breath caught sharply. Her eyes fell on the gleaming large abdominal wall retractor on the instrument table, and her teeth began to chatter beyond her control.

**[015]**
But then she thought of her old rival from police academy—the ever-smiling Yoriko Nikaido of Bokuto Station—and her pride as a young lady surged back into her throat.

**[016]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"N-no, I'm not changing my mind! Why would I? I heard that Yoriko Nikaido came here for an operation before, and Nurse Asakura looked after her too, right?"

**[017]**
As Miyuki leisurely fastened the broad positioning straps around Chie's wrists and ankles, she answered with a smile.

**[018]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"That's right. I handled Miss Nikaido's preoperative preparation and circulated during her operation as well. I had no idea you two police officers had such a connection."

**[019]**
Chie tilted her neck back. Her voice wavered, but she kept trying to save face.

**[020]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"Hmph! Nikaido needed anesthesia for a little operation, but my physical condition and willpower are far better than hers. A mere four or five hours of open abdominal surgery without anesthesia? I won't even furrow my brow!"

**[021]**
She paused, then could not help lowering her voice as she stole a pleading glance at Miyuki.

**[022]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"...Um, while I'm asking—when Nikaido lay on this table, did she get so scared she cried?"

**[023]**
Miyuki laughed softly and, through her sterile glove, lightly tapped Chie's smooth, taut lower abdomen.

**[024]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Miss Nikaido? She had a few more questions beforehand, but once she was on the table, she was wonderfully cooperative. You, on the other hand, Miss Sagamiono... You say you're not afraid, but your abdomen is already as hard as bulletproof steel."

**[025]**
Miyuki bent down and reminded her with a smiling gaze:
"With your abdominal muscles this tense, when Dr. Sakaguchi makes that long incision and the deep retractors hold your abdominal wall apart for more than four hours, it will hurt twice as much, you know~"

**[026]**
Chie's face suddenly turned pale beneath the blush, and the fingers strapped to the armboards dug hard into the edges of the padding.

**[027]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"T-twice as much?! Stop trying to scare me! I'm not tensing up... The operating table is just so cold I can't help it!"

**[028]**
Ryuji had already begun swabbing her flat lower abdomen with gauze soaked in povidone-iodine, disinfecting a broad area from below the xiphoid process toward the pubic symphysis. The cool, damp touch made Chie gasp, and tears immediately welled in her eyes.

**[029]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"Wait... wait! Are you really going to cut me open with nothing to dull it for more than four hours?! Isn't there any way to distract me that doesn't count as anesthesia?! If I cry and beg you to stop here today and Nikaido hears about it, I may as well resign!"

**[030]**
Ryuji paused in his disinfection and glanced at Miyuki.

**[031A]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Asakura, last time you asked me to call you in for a major operation without anesthesia, didn't you say you had a special way to reassure the patient?"

**[032A]**
Miyuki blinked above her mask, delight in her voice as if she had been waiting for this.

**[033A]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"When the surgeon in charge asks, how could I keep it to myself?"

**[031B]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"It isn't shameful to change your mind about anesthesia now, Miss Sagamiono. Pride alone won't get you through four or five hours of open exploration and resection."

**[032B]**
Chie bit her lip and shook her head desperately. "Never! If I take anesthesia, that means I've lost to Nikaido!"

**[033B]**
Miyuki laughed quietly beside her. "If Officer Sagamiono is that brave, I suppose I'll have to offer a little special nursing care."

**[034]**
Miyuki turned to the circulating nurse's cart and took a small pink object with a compact wireless speed controller out of its own sealed box; its surface had already been cleaned and wrapped.

**[035]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Miss Sagamiono, since you won't use anesthesia, I happen to have a little something specifically for 'taking your mind off things.' Would you like to try it?"

**[036]**
Miyuki bent close to Chie's ear and calmly explained where the device would be placed and how it worked. The switch and speed dial would stay entirely in Chie's own hand; it would run only if she chose to turn it on, and she could pause or stop it herself at any time.

**[037]**
Chie froze as though struck by lightning. The flush on her face spread to her ears and collarbones.

**[038]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"Whaaat?! What nonsense are you talking about, Nurse Asakura?! Th-this is a serious operating room, isn't it?! How could I possibly use that... that strange little thing to distract myself during major abdominal surgery?!"

**[039]**
Miyuki straightened and spread her hands with a look of innocent concern.

**[040]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"It's an entirely optional personal comfort item. If you don't need it, I'll put it away and let Dr. Sakaguchi drape you and make the incision. After all, four hours will pass in no time—as long as you can keep from crying that whole time~"

**[041]**
Playing along, Ryuji picked up the sterile fenestrated abdominal drape. The young nurse lifted the tray holding a round-bladed scalpel, and metal rang against metal.

**[042]**
Chie looked at the gleaming scalpel. A dreadful image of the next four hours—her abdomen cut open, her intestines moved and pulled—flashed through her mind. Then she looked at the wireless controller in Miyuki's hand. Embarrassment and self-preservation battled across her face for three seconds.

**[043]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"...W-wait!! Who said I didn't want it?!"

**[044]**
Chie shut her eyes and, mortified, reached out to snatch the little wireless controller. She forced the words through clenched teeth.

**[045]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"Fine, I'll use it!! I-it isn't an anesthetic drug, anyway. If I get through this major operation without anesthesia, I still beat Nikaido! Hurry up and put it in place—and don't look over there!!"

**[046]**
Before the sterile drape covered the area below her abdomen, Miyuki quickly and properly positioned the aid outside the sterile field and tucked the controller into Chie's right palm.

**[047]**
The young nurse stared in disbelief. While helping lay out the green sterile drapes, she quietly tugged Miyuki's scrub sleeve and whispered:

**[048]**
Young Nurse:
"Nurse Asakura, is this really all right?"

**[049]**
Miyuki turned toward her. Above the mask, her eyes curved into smiling crescents, revealing a rare hint of her mischievous side as she replied softly:

**[050]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"It really works. Trust me."

**[051]**
Once the final fenestrated drape was in place, sterile sheets separated Chie's upper chest and lower limbs from view. Only the abdominal field, coated in golden povidone-iodine, remained exposed directly beneath the surgical lights.

**[052]**
Ryuji accepted the scalpel from the young nurse and glanced toward the head of the table.

**[053]**
Standing at Chie's head, Miyuki gently pressed her shoulder and nodded to Ryuji.

**[054]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"The patient's vital signs are stable, and her secret weapon is ready too. Begin, Dr. Sakaguchi."

**[055]**
The moment the blade descended along the midline, Chie gasped sharply. Her right thumb pushed the dial in her palm up almost by instinct.

**[056]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"Nngh—!! It hurts, it hurts... No, that's strange... Aah...!"

**[057]**
For the next four hours, the atmosphere in Operating Room One became extraordinarily awkward and absurd.

**[058]**
Separating the widespread intestinal adhesions demanded painstaking sharp dissection. Each time Ryuji adjusted the teeth of the self-retaining abdominal retractor to widen the field, or used warm saline-soaked gauze to ease a loop of small intestine aside and expose the deep retroperitoneal cyst, Chie could clearly feel the pull on her organs while fully conscious.

**[059]**
At every such critical moment, Chie would bite her lip, flush bright red, and grip the controller in her hand for dear life.

**[060]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"Hah... Are you still moving my intestines around...? It feels sore and stretched inside my abdomen... but wh-why is everything below so numb I can't put any strength into it... Nngh...!"

**[061]**
The young nurse at the instrument table had started out so nervous that her palms were sweating. But after passing several long-handled hemostats and saline-soaked pads, she discovered something astonishing: the violent breath-holding and reflexive abdominal resistance so common in patients without anesthesia dissolved, every few seconds, into a soft, trembling breath from Chie.

**[062]**
Miyuki kept watch on the heart-rate and blood-pressure traces on the monitor while wiping the sweat from Chie's cheeks with warm, damp gauze. She still could not resist gently adding fuel to the fire.

**[063]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Two and a half hours have passed, Officer Sagamiono. Dr. Sakaguchi is dissecting the deepest part of the cyst wall next to the abdominal aorta now. If the pulling is too much, you can turn that switch in your hand up one more notch~"

**[064]**
With involuntary tears at the corners of her eyes, Chie glared at her through a dazed, indignant haze.

**[065]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"Q-quit talking...! I already turned it all the way up...! Ngh... Dr. Sakaguchi, hurry... If this goes on—hurting and tingling at the same time—my brain is going to burn out...!"

**[066]**
Near the end of the third hour, Ryuji lifted the fully dissected retroperitoneal cyst out of the abdomen and began thoroughly irrigating the peritoneal cavity with large amounts of warm saline. The warm current stirred an odd, rippling sensation inside the open abdomen.

**[067]**
Chie shuddered all over, nearly dropping the controller. A sound escaped her nose that even she could not tell was a cry of pain or a mortified gasp, and she sank limply into the operating table's padding.

**[068]**
Under the surgical lights, Ryuji was steadily closing the abdomen layer by layer. He looked up and met Miyuki's gaze.

**[069]**
Behind their tied surgical masks, they exchanged a knowing look. Mischievous triumph shone in Miyuki's eyes; and Ryuji had to admit that, absurd as the device was, this major operation without anesthesia—one that should have been exceedingly difficult to coordinate—had proceeded remarkably smoothly because of it.

**[070]**
Four hours and forty minutes later, the final skin suture was cut and a long sterile dressing applied. The lengthy major open abdominal operation without anesthesia was finally and successfully over.

**[071]**
Before she was covered again with a transfer sheet and wheeled back to the ward, a nearly exhausted Chie clung to Miyuki's sleeve. She gave a feeble but fierce warning.

**[072]**
Chie Sagamiono:
"L-listen... I didn't have a single drop of anesthesia today. I got through four hours and forty minutes of major open abdominal surgery fair and square! As for that... that pink thing—if you let Yoriko Nikaido hear one word about it, I'll haunt you even after I'm dead...!"

**[073]**
[Transition: corridor outside the surgical scrub sinks]
After seeing the patient off, completing the instrument count, and filing the operative record, the clock had reached two in the afternoon. Five full hours of demanding work were finally behind them. Ryuji removed his mask outside the scrub sinks and saw Miyuki walking out, humming in an excellent mood.

**[074]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"...Where did you even get that thing you used with the patient today?"

**[075]**
Miyuki undid the ties behind her head and removed her mask, revealing her fair face and a rather pleased smile.

**[076]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Heh-heh. That's my specially prepared 'secret wonder tool for the operating room.'"

**[077]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Only you could come up with that. Getting a policewoman through more than four hours of major open abdominal surgery without anesthesia by distracting her with one of those things... I don't know anyone else who'd dare."

**[078]**
Miyuki tilted her head and smiled as if the answer were obvious.

**[079]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Didn't it work out well? We didn't violate sterile procedure; the patient understood and consented, and she held the switch and controlled it herself. It saved the surgeon the trouble of a tense abdominal wall, too."

**[080]**
As she spoke, her eyes lingered for two seconds on Ryuji's long, steady fingers. Her voice dropped meaningfully.

**[081]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Still, watching you cut someone open and work on her for more than four hours... It left me feeling a little restless myself."

## Part B — My Turn on the Table

**[082]**
After the evening shift handover, half the ceiling lights in the surgical wing's corridor were switched off. Only the emergency exit sign cast its quiet green glow.

**[083]**
Ryuji had just locked his office door and was about to leave when a hand reached around the corridor corner and lightly caught the sleeve of his white coat.

**[084]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Caught you, Dr. Sakaguchi."

**[085]**
Miyuki stepped out of the shadows. She had not yet changed out of her fitted white nurse's uniform, and her lips curved in that familiar sweet, slightly teasing smile.

**[086]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Miyuki? Didn't you finish your shift a while ago?"

**[087]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"I did. But I saw on the schedule that Operating Room One will be empty tonight after its terminal cleaning, and there's no emergency setup booked for tomorrow morning."

**[088]**
Miyuki moved half a step closer. Her fingers slid from Ryuji's sleeve to his wrist and gave it a gentle shake.

**[089]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Ryuji, it isn't too late yet, is it? Give me half an hour and come somewhere with me."

**[090]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Where? It isn't another off-site club that needs a reservation, is it?"

**[091]**
Miyuki laughed and held an index finger to her lips in a hush gesture.

**[092]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Didn't you complain last time that the sets and toys at that place weren't realistic enough?"

**[093]**
Miyuki turned and placed her slender fingertips on the heavy automatic door switch for Operating Room One. She looked back and winked at Ryuji.

**[094]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"If we're after realism... what better ready-made stage could there be than the place we work in every day?"

**[095]**
The sensor door chimed softly and slid open. Miyuki took Ryuji by the wrist and stepped into the dim, quiet operating room.

**[096]**
The sensor door closed behind them, shutting out the last sounds of the corridor.

**[097]**
The room, its main lights still off, carried a faint disinfectant smell. A clean pale-green sterile sheet covered the operating table in the center, while every monitor around it sat in dark standby.

**[098]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"If Nurse Manager Ishigami catches us on her rounds, you won't be sleeping in tomorrow."

**[099]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Don't worry. Nurse Manager Ishigami went to the administration building for the monthly safety meeting half an hour ago. I checked her route before I came to intercept you."

**[100]**
Miyuki released Ryuji's hand and walked lightly to the operating table. She tapped the lighting panel by the wall, switching on only the surgical lights directly above it.

**[101]**
A cool, brilliant column of light poured down, revealing every detail of the table while making the rest of the room seem darker and more private.

**[102]**
Instead of taking her usual place as a scrub or circulating nurse, Miyuki braced her hands on the table's edge, hopped up, removed her white nursing shoes, and lay back in its center.

**[103]**
Ryuji paused in surprise, then walked to the table's side and looked down at her.

**[104]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"...What are you doing?"

**[105]**
Miyuki tucked her hands behind her head. As she lay flat, the skirt of her white nurse's uniform slid up slightly, showing a little of her well-shaped fair calves. She tipped her face up and smiled at Ryuji through the glare of the surgical lights.

**[106]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Can't you tell? I'm not playing the nurse today. It's my turn to be your patient."

**[107]**
Miyuki brought her hands down from behind her head and laid them on the armboards, deliberately slipping her wrists beneath the soft nylon positioning straps.

**[108]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Come on, surgeon. I'm always the one positioning your female patients and fastening their wrist straps. Why don't you try fastening mine today?"

**[109A]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Aren't you afraid I'll really tighten them? You won't be able to get down if you change your mind."

**[110A]**
Miyuki raised an eyebrow. Instead of drawing her wrist back, she lifted it a little higher.

**[111A]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"My, Dr. Sakaguchi sounds so imposing. But you know you'd release me whenever I said stop, don't you?"

**[109B]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"What made you decide to switch places today? At that off-site club last time, you were still the one holding the examination form and directing me."

**[110B]**
Miyuki blinked, a sly curve forming at the corner of her mouth.

**[111B]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Even a surgeon gets tired of playing the same way every time. Besides, I can't just talk about it without ever trying it."

**[112]**
Ryuji laughed. Taking her slender wrists in his hands, he fastened the broad nylon straps on either side only loosely, leaving enough room for her to pull free whenever she wished.

**[113]**
Even so, at the faint click of the metal buckles, Miyuki's shoulders gave an almost imperceptible tremble.

**[114]**
She looked up at the great disk of surgical lights overhead. Her breathing grew a shade quicker, and a faint blush rose in her cheeks.

**[115]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"...So this is how dazzling that light looks from below."

**[116]**
Ryuji planted his hands on the table's edge and leaned into her view, blocking the harshest beam from above.

**[117]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Now do you understand why Miss Sagamiono and the others get nervous as soon as they lie here?"

**[118]**
Miyuki looked at Ryuji close above her. Her eyes shifted, and she suddenly laughed softly.

**[119]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"My heart is beating a little faster. But... after being the S for so long, I've wanted to know what being the M feels like."

**[120]**
Miyuki tipped her head slightly. With her fingers loosely held on the armboard, she gave the hem of Ryuji's coat a light tug.

**[121]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Come to think of it, nearly every day I undress other girls in the ward, prepare them, and bring them to this table. I watch them blush and tremble under the surgical lights... And yet I've never tried lying here myself."

**[122]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"You've never sneaked in and tried it alone?"

**[123]**
Miyuki immediately shot him a look and protested with perfect conviction.

**[124]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Of course not! What would be the point of lying alone on this cold metal table? I'd be doing performance art like Dr. Miyama!"

**[125]**
She paused, her voice softening with a playful cunning she showed only to her lover.

**[126]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"I only dared to get up here today because I brought you with me. If anything happens, I can use you as my shield."

**[127]**
Miyuki raised one leg slightly, brushing her knee almost imperceptibly against Ryuji's side. An undisguised invitation spread through her eyes.
"Since I'm already lying here... Ryuji, aren't you going to give me a thorough 'preoperative examination' tonight?"

**[128]**
Ryuji did not banter back. He unfastened the loose nylon straps around her wrists, bent down, and kissed the lips that so loved to tease him.

**[129]**
Miyuki gave a muffled little sound. Her arms rose from the armboards and wrapped around Ryuji's neck with practiced warmth and urgency.

**[130]**
[Image slowly fades to black / action suggested offscreen]
In the quiet operating room, the surgical lights cast their cool glow over a stethoscope set aside and the slightly disordered collar of a white nurse's uniform. Miyuki, usually so effortless in setting the pace, gradually lost control of her breathing on the narrow operating table. Biting her lip, she could only watch the man above her from the corner of her flushed eyes as she yielded, little by little, to a position both familiar and unfamiliar...

**[131]**
[Image fades back in]
A little later, the operating room was quiet again.

**[132]**
Miyuki leaned against Ryuji, the flush not yet gone from her cheeks. She slowly buttoned the last two buttons at the collar of her nurse's uniform and smoothed her slightly disheveled hair.

**[133]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Phew... Fooling around on a real operating table does make my heart race much more than that themed place outside."

**[134]**
Sitting on the edge of the table, she swung her calves and recovered her usual sweet, slightly mischievous smile. But there was a faint note of jealousy in the look she gave Ryuji.

**[135]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"But speaking of that, Ryuji... I've heard that other women on the hospital staff have actually been wheeled here naked from the ward, just like Miss Sagamiono, and lain on this table for you to operate on."

**[136]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Those were clinical surgery training cases formally approved by the director—"

**[137]**
Miyuki held a finger against Ryuji's lips, smiling as she interrupted his serious explanation.

**[138]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"I didn't say I was going to report you. Why are you in such a hurry?"

**[139]**
She withdrew her hand and leaned back on her palms against the green drape behind her. Tilting her head slightly, she fixed Ryuji with a direct, earnest gaze.

**[140]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"What I want to ask is... next time, would you operate on me too?"

**[141]**
Ryuji paused in surprise.
"...Are you serious? You don't mean role-playing with your clothes on, like tonight?"

**[142]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Of course I'm serious. Ever since I watched you finish Miss Sagamiono's major open abdominal operation without anesthesia, I've kept thinking about it."

**[143]**
Her fingertips tapped lightly on the operating table beneath her. Her voice held both the clear judgment of a veteran nurse and her own distinctive bold curiosity.
"Being undressed completely in the ward, fully prepared and wheeled here, then entrusting my body to you to cut open while I'm wide awake without anesthesia... I don't want to leave that ultimate act of surrendering control and trust to someone else for other people alone to experience."

**[144A]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"Don't be too confident. When the time comes, if we start with ward preparation, follow the rules, and have you sign the consent form and lie here, my scalpel won't stop even if you cry and clutch the sheet and beg for mercy."

**[145A]**
Miyuki's cheeks warmed, but her eyes sparkled as she smiled. "Then I'll have to see whether your hand is steadier—or whether you'll feel sorry for me first."

**[144B]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"This isn't like tonight's play. If you really want to try it, we'll follow the hospital's formal process and discuss the procedure, anesthesia, and preoperative preparation one by one."

**[145B]**
Miyuki nodded with a smile. "As you say, surgeon. I wasn't planning to have you operate on me empty-handed tonight."

**[146]**
Ryuji put an arm around her waist and lifted her safely down from the operating table.

**[147]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"All right. I promise. Once we find the right opening in the schedule, I'll operate on you myself."

**[148]**
Miyuki put on her white nurse's shoes and looked up at him from the floor, her eyes full of satisfaction. She was no prey tamed by someone else; she had chosen to entrust the most important position to the man she trusted most.

**[149]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"It's a promise, then, my surgeon."
She switched off the surgical lights overhead, naturally linked her arm with Ryuji's, and together they left the operating room to its quiet once more.

## Part C — The Capable Do More

**[150]**
The nurses' station in the ward was as busy as ever during the day.

**[151]**
Ryuji approached with a chart of orders he had just signed and saw Chizuru Ishigami set a thick stack of rotation assessment forms in front of Miyuki.

**[152]**
Chizuru Ishigami:
"Asakura, you'll be responsible for teaching the two young nurses transferring here next week how to prepare patients in the ward and count instruments."

**[153]**
Miyuki immediately put on a flawless, sweet professional smile.

**[154]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Of course, Nurse Manager Ishigami. Leave it to me."

**[155]**
Chizuru gave a small nod and walked briskly away from the nurses' station.

**[156]**
Almost the very second Chizuru disappeared around the corridor corner, Miyuki's sweet smile collapsed. With a long sigh, she slumped bonelessly over the station counter.

**[157]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"Sigh... Somebody save me..."

**[158]**
Ryuji stepped up to the counter and tapped it, amused.

**[159]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"You sounded pretty willing just now."

**[160]**
Miyuki looked up and rolled her eyes at him, jabbing her ballpoint pen into the thick stack of training forms as she complained.

**[161]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"How could I say no in front of Nurse Manager Ishigami? Honestly, I came to Hoshimi Hospital to be an easygoing junior nurse because I was sick of that old semi-management work—supervising people, making schedules, and training new staff..."

**[162]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"And look what happened. I barely managed to pretend I had no experience for a few days, and now whenever Nurse Manager Ishigami has the thankless job of training newcomers, she dumps it on me."

**[163]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"That's what you get for being unable to hide how skilled you are. The capable do more."

**[164]**
Miyuki Asakura:
"You're teasing me too!"

**[165]**
Resting her chin on her hand, she lowered her voice to complain to Ryuji.
"You have no idea. Some of the new nurses are so clumsy they can tie even the simplest sterile pack into an impossible knot. It makes me so angry I want to strap them to the operating table and make an incision so they'll learn their lesson."

**[166]**
Ryuji stopped short and looked at her, momentarily at a loss for words.

**[167]**
Ryuji Sakaguchi:
"............"

**[168]**
Seeing Ryuji's frozen expression, Miyuki stared for half a second, then burst out laughing. She reached across the counter and lightly patted the back of his hand.
"I'm joking! Dr. Sakaguchi, what kind of face is that? Don't tell me you actually believed me?"

**[169]**
Ryuji looked at her lovely eyes, curved like crescents yet always keeping him guessing how much mischief lay behind them, and sighed helplessly.
"I think you really would do it."
