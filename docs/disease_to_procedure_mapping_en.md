# Disease-to-Procedure Guide

This guide lists the operation associated with each diagnosis in the current standard case pool. Use the diagnosis shown in the patient chart to find the expected procedure.

> The mapping reflects the current game data. It is a gameplay reference, not real-world medical advice.

## General and Abdominal Surgery

| Diagnosis | Select this procedure | Why this matches |
|---|---|---|
| Acute appendicitis | **Open Appendectomy** | Removes the inflamed appendix. |
| Acute peritonitis to be investigated | **Exploratory Laparotomy** | Opens the abdomen to identify and treat the source of widespread peritoneal inflammation. |
| Calculus acute cholecystitis | **Open Cholecystectomy** | Removes the inflamed gallbladder containing gallstones. |
| Reducible inguinal hernia | **Inguinal Hernia Repair** | Repairs the groin hernia defect. |
| Symptomatic incisional abdominal wall hernia | **Ventral Hernia Repair** | Repairs a hernia through a previous abdominal incision or the ventral abdominal wall. |
| Gastric adenocarcinoma of the antrum | **Open Partial Gastrectomy** | Removes the distal part of the stomach containing the antral tumor. |
| Proximal gastric adenocarcinoma | **Open Total Gastrectomy** | Removes the entire stomach when the tumor is located proximally. |
| Right colon adenocarcinoma | **Open Right Hemicolectomy** | Removes the right side of the colon and its regional blood supply and lymphatic tissue. |
| Sigmoid colon adenocarcinoma | **Open Sigmoid Colectomy** | Removes the sigmoid colon segment containing the tumor. |
| Low rectal adenocarcinoma | **Open Abdominoperineal Resection** | Removes the low rectum and anus when sphincter-preserving resection is not suitable. |
| Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma of the head of the pancreas | **Open Pancreaticoduodenectomy** | Removes the pancreatic head and adjacent structures; this is the Whipple procedure. |
| Localized hepatocellular carcinoma | **Open Hepatic Lobectomy** | Removes the liver lobe containing the localized tumor. |
| Localized space-occupying lesion of the spleen | **Open Splenectomy** | Removes the spleen containing the lesion. |

## Breast Surgery

| Diagnosis | Select this procedure | Why this matches |
|---|---|---|
| Localized breast malignancy | **Breast Tumor Excision** | Removes the localized breast tumor while preserving the remainder of the breast. |
| Multifocal breast malignancy | **Total Mastectomy** | Removes the entire breast when malignant disease is present in multiple sites. |

## Gynecologic Surgery

| Diagnosis | Select this procedure | Why this matches |
|---|---|---|
| Symptomatic ovarian cystic neoplasm | **Open Ovarian Cystectomy** | Removes the ovarian cystic lesion while preserving ovarian tissue when possible. |
| Symptomatic uterine leiomyoma — uterus-preserving case | **Open Myomectomy** | Removes the fibroids while preserving the uterus. |
| Symptomatic uterine leiomyoma — definitive-treatment case | **Open Total Hysterectomy** | Removes the uterus as definitive treatment. Check the case's planned procedure and treatment goal because this diagnosis appears in two case types. |

## Thoracic and Cardiac Surgery

| Diagnosis | Select this procedure | Why this matches |
|---|---|---|
| Multivessel atherosclerotic heart disease | **Open-Chest Heart Bypass** | Bypasses multiple obstructed coronary arteries. |
| Localized non-small cell lung cancer | **Open-Chest Lung Lobectomy** | Removes the lung lobe containing a localized tumor. |
| Central non-small cell lung cancer | **Open-Chest Pneumonectomy** | Removes the affected lung when a central tumor cannot be treated adequately with lobectomy. |
| Thoracic esophageal squamous cell carcinoma | **Open-Chest Esophagectomy** | Removes the thoracic esophageal segment containing the cancer. |

## Urologic and Vascular Surgery

| Diagnosis | Select this procedure | Why this matches |
|---|---|---|
| Muscle-invasive bladder urothelial carcinoma | **Open Radical Cystectomy** | Removes the bladder for muscle-invasive disease. |
| Localized renal cell carcinoma | **Open Nephrectomy** | Removes the affected kidney containing the localized tumor. |
| Abdominal aortic aneurysm with surgical indications | **Open Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm Repair** | Replaces or reinforces the aneurysmal segment of the abdominal aorta. |

## Procedures Outside the Standard Case Pool

The following procedures exist in the surgery catalog but are not currently selected by any standard disease case template:

- **Total Vaginal Hysterectomy**
- **Open Artificial-Heart System Exchange**

They may appear in special or advanced event content rather than ordinary generated cases.

## Similar Names to Watch

- **Open Partial Gastrectomy** is for the current **antral** gastric cancer case; **Open Total Gastrectomy** is for the current **proximal** gastric cancer case.
- **Breast Tumor Excision** is for a **localized** breast malignancy; **Total Mastectomy** is for a **multifocal** breast malignancy.
- **Open-Chest Lung Lobectomy** is for the current **localized** lung cancer case; **Open-Chest Pneumonectomy** is for the current **central** lung cancer case.
- **Inguinal Hernia Repair** treats a groin hernia; **Ventral Hernia Repair** treats the current incisional abdominal wall hernia case.
- **Open Myomectomy** preserves the uterus; **Open Total Hysterectomy** removes it. Both currently use symptomatic uterine leiomyoma as the diagnosis, so confirm the case's treatment goal.
