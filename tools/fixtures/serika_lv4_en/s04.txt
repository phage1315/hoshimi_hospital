At daybreak, Serika was still sitting on the bench outside the monitored ward. She held a hot drink from the hospital vending machine without opening it.
Sakaguchi emerged from the ward, carrying the handover notes he had just completed.
“She's stable now, and the anesthetic is gradually wearing off. We need to keep monitoring for bleeding, infection, and other injuries.”
“…Her uterus?”
“Preserved. There's no new bleeding at the repair for now. I'll keep following her postoperative examinations.”
Serika's grip on the can loosened a little.
“…Thank you.”
“I should thank you. Without those judgments of yours, the operation would have been harder.”
“Don't say that yet. She hasn't woken up.”
“Right. Once she's fully conscious, we'll explain everything to her.”
Serika lowered her head and finally opened the drink. A little steam rose from the opening.
“I need to notify someone.”
“Who?”
“Miyuki Asakura. She knows Nanami too.”
Serika took out her phone and dialed a familiar number. When the call connected, she did not speak immediately.
“Hello? Serika? What's happened this early?”
“Miyuki… I've found Nanami.”
“…Who?”
“Nanami Horiuchi. She was brought in late last night.”
“…How is she?”
“Seriously injured. We've just finished operating.”
The other end of the line fell silent for a long while.
“…Is she alive?”
“She is.”
“…Thank goodness.”
“I'll visit her today. Get some rest first. Don't try to bear it all alone.”
“…All right.”
After hanging up, Serika glanced at Sakaguchi.
“Was I… useless back there?”
“You finished a very difficult operation.”
“I mean afterward.”
“You don't have to keep standing so straight once the operation's over.”
Serika looked at him, then slowly nodded after a few seconds.
“I've handed everything over to the next shift. Let me take you home.”
“You've been on duty all night.”
“I've handed over now. We both need rest.”
They each changed back into their everyday clothes and left the hospital. Commuters were already on the streets, and a breakfast shop had just raised its shutters.
“…How strange.”
“What is?”
“It feels as though hardly any time has passed. But it's already morning outside.”
Sakaguchi walked her to her door. Serika found her keys in her bag, then paused.
“Thank you… for walking me home today.”
“Get some sleep first. I'll tell you when Nanami wakes up.”
“…All right.”
She opened the door and glanced back before going inside. Sakaguchi was still standing where she had left him.
“Sakaguchi-kun.”
“Yes?”
“…You get some proper rest too.”
The door closed softly. Sakaguchi remained outside for a moment before turning to leave.
