Nanami was transferred onto the operating table. Satsuki Orikawa checked the ECG and blood-pressure equipment at the circulating nurse's monitoring position, while Shiori Yoneda verified the transfusion line nearby. Itsumi Sonoda and Rumi Iwasaki donned sterile gowns and prepared the instrument table.
“Anesthesia preparation and airway protection are complete. Her circulation is very unstable, and the transfusion is continuing. I'll handle monitoring and report any change immediately.”
“Good. You manage her circulation. I'll control the bleeding as quickly as possible.”
After helping confirm the deep injuries and imaging findings before surgery, Serika returned to Sakaguchi's side. She had committed the extent of the damage she had seen to memory.
“The damage is deep. Its shape suggests penetration by a hard foreign object, not an ordinary mucosal tear. It also involves the side of the cervix. We'll have to wait until she wakes to learn what happened.”
“The bleeding won't stop, and there's blood in the abdomen. I'll open her abdomen to find the source.”
“I'll help identify the injuries to the uterus and surrounding structures, and adjust the field as you direct.”
Serika had scrubbed and was waiting outside the sterile area. Shiori Yoneda, the second circulating nurse, helped her into a sterile gown and tied the straps behind her.
She slipped her hands into sterile gloves. Halfway into the first glove, her fingertips paused briefly.
“Dr. Misono?”
“…I'm fine.”
She pulled the glove into place and approached the operating table. Beyond the sterile drapes, Nanami's face was out of sight.
“Dr. Misono. Ready?”
“Ready.”
The surgical team verified the patient's temporary identity, emergency indications, planned procedure, blood supply, and everyone's responsibilities, confirming that all was correct.
“Let's begin.”
After making the lower abdominal incision, Sakaguchi entered the abdomen layer by layer. Suction removed the pooled blood, gradually revealing the deep pelvis.
“On the right. The hematoma ends here. Don't pull on that layer yet.”
“I see it. Suction, and raise the field a little.”
Serika adjusted the retractor, avoiding visibly congested tissue. Itsumi Sonoda passed a hemostatic instrument into Sakaguchi's hand.
“Hemostat. Suction is beside your right hand.”
“The tear runs from deep in the vagina to the side of the cervix. The outer part of the lower uterine segment is injured too. An adjacent vascular branch is torn, and blood keeps seeping deeper.”
“So even with external pressure, she's still bleeding inside.”
“The transfusion is continuing. Her blood pressure is barely holding, but we can't wait much longer.”
“Control the main bleeding point first.”
Sakaguchi clamped the bleeding vascular branch while Serika stabilized the tissue beside it. When suction moved away, they could finally see clearly into the wound.
“The uterine body still has a blood supply. The damage is concentrated in the lower segment and to the side.”
“The cervix and deep vaginal injury need layered repair too. We can't just close the part visible from outside.”
“Yes. The internal wound has to be addressed as well.”
Sakaguchi attempted to treat a deeper bleeding point, but the tissue was extremely fragile. Seeping blood obscured the view they had just gained.
“Her blood pressure is dropping again.”
“Continue transfusion. Dr. Misono, help me hold this steady.”
“The additional blood unit has been checked and is being connected. I'll keep recording the transfused volume.”
“All right.”
Serika moved swiftly and precisely. She left the bleeding point Sakaguchi was treating untouched, gently moving aside only the adjacent tissue that obstructed his view.
“This tear is deeper than we thought before surgery.”
“If both sides keep bleeding, repairing the lower uterine segment will get increasingly difficult.”
Sakaguchi glanced at the monitor, then back at the bleeding that had yet to stop.
“If local hemostasis and repair both fail, we'll have to prepare for a hysterectomy. That's the last option to save her life.”
Serika's grip on the retractor tightened slightly.
“…Please wait.”
“Do you see another option?”
“Yes. The tissue here isn't all necrotic. We can address the lateral bleeding points separately, and there are still edges we can suture in the lower uterine tear.”
“But the field is very narrow. Further laterally, we also have to avoid the ureter.”
“I know. I'd like to dissect along the intact tissue planes first, identify the ureter and vessels, and then attempt repair.”
Sakaguchi did not reply immediately. He reassessed the tissue color and the extent of the oozing.
“Can it be done?”
“…There's a chance. At least from what we can see now, removal isn't our only option yet.”
“Her life comes first. If we can't control the bleeding, we can't keep taking risks.”
“I understand. If it won't stop, I won't oppose switching to a hysterectomy.”
Serika glanced at the patient's head beyond the drapes. Nanami remained asleep, unable to hear their discussion.
“But she's still alive. Whatever we can preserve… I want to preserve as much as we can for her.”
Sakaguchi examined the intact tissue remaining at both ends of the tear, then looked up at Serika.
“Find a safe plane and the main bleeding points first, then repair the deep tear layer by layer. I can help with this part of the lower uterus.”
“All right. Dr. Misono, let's try your approach. One step at a time. If I think the risk is too high at any point, we stop immediately.”
“Understood.”
“Fine forceps, a needle holder, and fine sutures are ready. Just say when you need them.”
Serika switched to finer instruments. Slowing down, she first identified the intact tissue boundary, then gently separated the two closely apposed layers.
“Spare instruments of the same specifications are laid out too, and the gauze has been replenished.”
“Forceps.”
The instrument was placed in Serika's palm. Her hand did not tremble, and her eyes never left the operative field.
“Here. The vascular branch comes in from this side. It isn't the one we thought earlier.”
“I see it. I'll ligate the bleeding point. Keep the field open.”
Sakaguchi secured the critical bleeding point. When suction moved away again, fresh blood no longer immediately flooded the surgical field.
“The next blood unit is connected. Blood usage and fluid balance are being recorded.”
“She still needs more blood, but her pressure is starting to rise.”
“We can see the deep tear clearly now. Continue the repair.”
Sakaguchi first treated the cervical and deep vaginal injuries. Serika assisted with retraction and identifying the boundaries, then repaired the lower uterine tear with Sakaguchi's confirmation. Itsumi handled the primary fine-instrument exchanges while Rumi assisted with counts and replenishing instruments.
“The next set of sutures is ready. I'm keeping track of the count.”
“One more stitch here. Don't pull too tightly.”
“Right. Let the edges come together naturally.”
Serika tied the knot and released the needle holder. Sakaguchi checked the tension at the repair and assessed the surrounding tissue's blood supply.
“Dr. Misono, what do you think?”
“No new active bleeding at present. The blood supply to the uterus and surrounding tissue has been preserved.”
“Her vital signs are more stable now. We can continue monitoring.”
Sakaguchi and Serika checked the pelvis, repaired areas, bladder, and adjacent structures once more, confirming no overlooked bleeding points or obvious associated injuries.
“Good. We can finish.”
“Starting the final count. I'll verify the needles and instruments.”
“They match the circulating record. Gauze, needles, and instruments all accounted for.”
After closing the incision and completing the instrument count, the operation was finally over. Nanami was taken to a monitored ward for continued observation.
Serika stood still, looking down at the gloves she had just removed. She reached to drop them into the waste bin, but fumbled them on the first try.
“Dr. Misono?”
Serika leaned against the wall behind her, her knees buckling slightly. Sakaguchi immediately stepped forward to support her.
“…I'm sorry.”
“No need to apologize. Sit down for a while.”
“I… I really thought, back there…”
She did not finish, only shook her head hard.
“Nanami's bleeding has stopped. She still needs monitoring, but she's past the most dangerous part.”
At last, Serika lowered her head. After a long while, she gave an almost inaudible reply.
“…Yes.”
