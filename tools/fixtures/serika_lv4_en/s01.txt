At eleven at night, only the rows of lights at the nurses' station still illuminated the hospital corridor. Returning from his ward rounds, Sakaguchi pushed open the lounge door.
Serika Misono sat by the window, looking at a cup of cold coffee on the table. Only when she heard the door open did she raise her eyes.
“Dr. Misono? You're still here this late?”
“…Dr. Sakaguchi.”
“Are you on duty tonight too?”
Serika shook her head.
“No. I've finished my work in radiology.”
“Then why haven't you gone home?”
“…There's nothing to do there anyway.”
She turned her paper cup halfway around, then stopped.
“At least there are people here. There's nothing wrong with staying a little longer.”
“Using the hospital to pass the time? That doesn't sound like the person you used to be.”
“You didn't seem like someone who would spend an entire afternoon visiting music shops with someone either.”
“…How long are you going to keep bringing that up?”
“Until you can pick out the bass.”
The corners of her mouth moved slightly before she turned back to the window.
“Your coffee's cold. Shall I get you another?”
“No need. Aren't you on duty?”
“Even the doctor on duty is allowed to drink coffee.”
“…Then I'd like mine hot.”
Sakaguchi walked toward the kettle. He had just picked up a paper cup when the pager in his pocket suddenly lit up.
The screen showed only two lines: Emergency admission. Female, severe bleeding, impaired consciousness. Surgery team, attend immediately.
“Sorry. I'll owe you that coffee.”
“An emergency?”
“Heavy bleeding. I need to go down and have a look.”
Sakaguchi had already turned away. Behind him came the soft scrape of a chair sliding back.
“Dr. Sakaguchi.”
“Yes?”
“I'm coming too.”
“Aren't you off duty?”
“Finishing my work in radiology doesn't mean I can't help now.”
She picked up the pen from her white coat pocket, fastened her coat again, and was already at the door.
“All right. Let's see the patient first.”
They headed for the elevator, one behind the other. The empty paper cup he had just taken out remained beside the kettle in the lounge.
