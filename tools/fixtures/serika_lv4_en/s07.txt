Morning light filtered through the curtains into the room. When Sakaguchi woke, the pillow beside him was empty.
From the living room came the soft sound of a cup being set on a table. Sakaguchi walked out and found Serika already dressed, preparing coffee.
“Morning.”
“Morning. When did you get up?”
“Not long ago. Last night… I slept very well.”
She handed Sakaguchi a cup of coffee and sat at the table with her own.
“How are you feeling physically?”
“…Is that your doctor's occupational habit again?”
“Just asking.”
“I'm fine. My eyes are a little swollen, that's all.”
She touched the corner of her eye, then lifted her face toward Sakaguchi.
“…Yesterday, you saw me in a dreadful state.”
“I didn't think it was dreadful.”
“…Mm.”
Serika lowered her head, took a sip of coffee, and paused.
“Nanami asked me to go back and see my father. You heard her too.”
“I did. Have you decided?”
“Not yet. I don't want to make an immediate decision at a time like this.”
“That hospital, my father, and Miyuki… There are so many things I still owe it to myself to say.”
“You can take your time.”
“…But there's one thing I've made up my mind about.”
She set down her coffee and sat a little straighter.
“I won't pretend I haven't seen what Shiozaki has done anymore.”
“Nanami has asked the hospital to contact the police. I'll testify too, and tell them what I know.”
“Yes.”
“And… Shiozaki once forced me to poison my father. I didn't actually do it, but I nearly obeyed him then. Miyuki was the one who took me away.”
“…Thank you for being willing to tell me.”
“…I've always been afraid. Afraid I might really become that kind of person one day.”
“But if I run away again, I won't even be able to trust myself afterward.”
Sakaguchi sat across from her, waiting for her to continue.
“For now, I still want to stay at Hoshimi. This place… is somewhere I've chosen for myself now.”
“Then stay. You can decide the next step when you're ready.”
Serika nodded gently. Her gaze left the window and settled on Sakaguchi's face again.
“…There's something else.”
“Yes?”
“About last night… I don't want you to think it was only because I was afraid to be alone.”
“I like you. I started liking you some time ago. I just never dared say it.”
“Serika… I like you too.”
Serika lowered her head, her ears turning slightly red. She touched the cup handle with a fingertip, then raised her eyes again.
“Then… are we a couple now?”
“If you want to be.”
“…Do you even need my formal confirmation for something like this?”
“I learned it at the hospital. Important things have to be decided by the person concerned.”
Serika paused, then laughed softly.
“…What a strange doctor.”
“I do. Sakaguchi-kun.”
“Then may I call you Serika outside the hospital from now on?”
“Didn't you already call me that last night?”
“I didn't get a chance to ask then.”
“…You may.”
She held her hand out to Sakaguchi. Once he took it, she did not immediately let go.
“But now that you're my boyfriend… there's one more thing.”
“What?”
“You still haven't turned in the bass homework you owe me.”
“…Are we having a test now?”
“No need. You can take your time.”
She gave Sakaguchi a small smile and carried their empty cups to the sink. Outside the window, morning had fully arrived.
Sakaguchi helped her pick up the coat beside the table. Together they gathered their things, ready to return to their respective posts at the hospital.
