Five thirty. When the nurse on duty knocks, Yoriko is already awake. She looks at the notebook beside her pillow and finds she has no desire to open it again.
“…Yesterday I was only listening to her. Today it's really my turn.”
“Good morning, Miss Nikaido. First, let's confirm your name and today's procedure.”
After a series of checks, cleaning and skin preparation, her hospital gown and personal clothes are put away, and a blanket is carefully laid over her. Her surgical cap contains all her hair; her glasses go into the belongings bag too.
“…Everything Miss Asakura said yesterday. Even the order is exactly right.”
Before Yoriko quite realizes it, she is lying on a trolley. One nurse pushes, another watches the lines, and handover confirmations echo along the corridor.
“Wait, wasn't I just in my room?”
“Yes. We're heading to the surgical area now. It's almost seven.”
“…Seven. So there are still two hours to go.”
Seven sharp. The operating-room door opens, and light reflects off the ceiling. Familiar eyes smile at her between a surgical cap and a mask.
“Good morning, Miss Nikaido. How did you sleep?”
“Miss Asakura! I dreamed you kept announcing the time… and now it really is seven!”
“Hehe. Then I'll be looking after you for the next two hours.”
“When you put it like that, it sounds like it's going to take forever again…”
Before she can dwell on it, the nurses are helping her onto the narrow operating table. Someone checks the position of her arms, someone connects the monitors, and someone checks the IV line.
“Ah, it really is narrow… Wait, was that the ECG? What's next?”
“The blood-pressure cuff and oxygen monitor. Someone over there was asking you to say your name just now too.”
“I heard! I'm turning into a machine that automatically answers with my own name!”
“Hehe. By the way, Miss Nikaido, how does it feel lying here?”
“This might sound strange… I'm lying here with nothing on, just a blanket, but…”
The ECG beeps steadily. Every so often the blood-pressure cuff gently tightens around her arm, then slowly releases. Yoriko listens intently.
“Those beeps, and the cuff tightening again and again… It all feels so immediate! Ah, I'm really in an operating room!”
“So you like that feeling. A little different from listening to the radio in a police car, isn't it?”
“Completely different! Usually I'm the one heading to the scene… Today I'm the person at its center. Huh, now that I say it, I'm even getting a little excited!”
Sayaka Nanjo comes to the table, confirms her name and explains the epidural. Asakura stays within earshot, ready to help adjust Yoriko's position.
“Miss Nikaido, I'm Nanjo, handling your anesthesia today. Turn onto your side with me, and curve your back a little… Yes, very good. Tell me if you're uncomfortable.”
“Ugh… Just hearing about this last night scared me. But now everyone's talking to me, I hardly have time to imagine things.”
“Then follow Dr. Nanjo's instructions and breathe slowly. We'll tell you clearly whenever we need your help.”
Seven forty. The epidural is complete. Nanjo checks the area of the block while the circulating nurse continues watching the monitors. Then come catheterization, positioning and rearranging the coverings; someone explains each action, and someone is always moving on to the next task.
“Just hearing you describe this part yesterday made my face burn… and today it's already finished?”
“Miss Asakura, something just occurred to me. Taking off all your clothes and going somewhere full of people you barely know… it's a bit like your first trip to a hot spring.”
“Hehe, a hot spring? Well, you did wash first, move somewhere else, and everyone around you treats it as perfectly natural.”
“Exactly! Just now I even thought that since everyone seems so used to it, maybe I don't need to feel so embarrassed.”
Yoriko instinctively tries to turn a little. The safety strap, arm supports and connected monitoring lines immediately remind her where she is lying.
“…Until I got tied up and couldn't move! W-wait, what hot spring ties its guests to lounge chairs?!”
“It's safety support to prevent slipping, not tying you up. Though… I doubt a hot spring has quite so many staff all attending to just you.”
“That makes perfect sense… so why doesn't it reassure me at all?!”
“You've been doing very well. Is anything uncomfortable now?”
“I'm a little cold, and… without my glasses, everyone's face is blurry.”
“All right, I'll adjust the warming. You may not see our faces clearly, but you can still recognize our voices, can't you?”
“I can! Especially one particular nurse who always sounds so happy describing frightening things!”
“Oh? Somehow that sounds like a compliment.”
After eight. The sound of instrument packs opening, Mika Nakai checking instrument and gauze counts, and doctors discussing images and the scope of surgery follows one after another. Yoriko looks left, only for someone on her right to call to her.
“What are they counting over there? …Ah, all right, I'll keep this hand still. Huh, are you adjusting the light now?”
“We're checking the instruments and gauze. Every count has to be exact. You can leave this part to me, Miss Nikaido.”
“You're very busy, Miss Nikaido.”
“You're the ones doing all the work! But it feels like someone comes over to see me every few moments…”
“Weren't you afraid of being surrounded by everyone last night?”
“I was… But it's strange. I thought I'd lie here nervous for two hours. It doesn't seem that long now.”
“We haven't skipped a single thing you were so frightened of last night. It's just that whenever you almost have time to be scared, someone else comes to see you.”
“Yesterday it sounded like a long, terrifying movie… Today it feels more like everyone's filming the same scene, and I'm the only one who hasn't learned her lines.”
“You already know your lines. Your name, your procedure, and telling us if something feels uncomfortable. We'll prompt you for the rest when the time comes.”
“Pfft… Put that way, I suppose you're right.”
Eight fifty. The sterile drapes are in place, and a blue screen blocks Yoriko's view of her abdomen. Sakaguchi, fully dressed for surgery, comes to the table.
“Good morning, Miss Nikaido. We've rechecked the anesthesia. Are you uncomfortable anywhere?”
“No obvious pain… Doctor, I didn't expect to see you so soon.”
“Soon? You've been in the operating room for almost two hours.”
“What?! Really? …It feels like someone's been talking to me the whole time, and it's gone by in a blink!”
Sakaguchi looks at Yoriko, then at Asakura, who is checking her monitors and coverings. Asakura's eyes curve in a smile, but she offers no explanation.
“Miss Nikaido, after all we talked about last night, do you still have any questions?”
“I do! Like whether they've already got the scalpel ready over there, and…”
Asakura leans down slightly and lowers her voice, as though sharing a secret just between them.
“Are you nervous, or thinking about something else… or a little of both?”
“…A little of both.”
“Hehe. When we got to this part last night, you pulled the blanket right up to your chin.”
“Don't tell the doctor about that!”
Sakaguchi catches half the exchange and seems about to ask what they discussed last night. But Asakura has already straightened up, professionally checking the identity, procedure, anesthesia and instrument readiness with the team.
“The block and monitoring are confirmed. Miss Nikaido, I'll be here throughout. Tell me immediately if you actually feel pain.”
As planned, Sakaguchi adds local infiltration around the incision site. The team completes the final time-out checks, their clear responses sounding through the operating room.
“Preparation is complete. Miss Nikaido, we're going to begin now.”
“Yes, all right… Huh?”
“Scalpel.”
Yoriko instinctively looks at the clock. Nine sharp. Mika Nakai passes the first scalpel to Sakaguchi. Behind the blue screen, the medical team is in position.
“W-wait! Are you really making the incision now?!”
Shrrk—.
“Ugh…”
