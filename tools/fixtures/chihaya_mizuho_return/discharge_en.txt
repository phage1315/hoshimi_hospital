"...You used to do exactly the same thing."

"And our very experienced Ms. Kuramoto shouldn't lift anything heavy either."
"...Yes, ma'am."

"Ow, ow... I guess I really shouldn't get carried away today."
"See?"
"But we're really leaving! When you were in hospital before, I could only visit. Now here we are, packing up together."
"And you actually ended up on an operating table too."
"Hehe. Another achievement for my life story."
"Please don't treat abdominal surgery like a school club achievement!"
"I'll finish the handover. Come to the nurses' station in a little while. Once we've checked the paperwork, you can leave the ward."
"Okay!"
"Thank you, Nurse Asakura!"
[Asakura picks up the records and leaves the room.]
[Chihaya takes her small, light bag, while Mizuho is empty-handed. At the doorway, they both turn for one last look at the room.]
"...Finally, no more sleeping here."
"But all the nurses were so nice to us."
"That's true."
"They still remember your third operation especially."
"...Don't bring that up!"
"I mean the time you walked into the operating room yourself."
"...That one's okay to talk about."
"Hahaha!"
[They walk slowly toward the nurses' station.]
[The handover at the nurses' station is complete. Asakura confirms that staff have taken both girls' luggage to the exit. Chihaya and Mizuho reach the corridor leading toward outpatients and radiology.]
"Great! Can we go straight out now?"
"...Wait a second."
"What? Did you leave something in the room?"
"Not something."
"Then what?"
"I want to see Dr. Misono first."
"...Oh!"
"She visited me after my third operation. But I've wanted to thank her again in person on the day I leave."
"I was just thinking of going to see her too!"
"Really?"
"Of course! She helped Dr. Sakaguchi with my operation from start to finish."
"And when you were most scared, she kept teaching you how to breathe."
"...Yeah."

"Still, remembering her walking up to the table with that marking pen gives me chills."
"Haha. Dr. Misono's really kind, though."
"You were talking to her about girls' schools and the light music club! I was lying there listening to her discuss blade sizes!"

"I know. I realized afterward that she really looks after people."
"Especially that last little gesture?"
"...Don't act like you know what I'm thinking!"
"'Dr. Daimon...'"
"Chi-ha-ya!"
[They both burst out laughing, then gently hold their abdomens.]
"...Ow. Maybe we should laugh less."
"Agreed. By the way, do you know how to get to radiology?"
"We just passed a sign. It should be down this corridor."
"Then lead the way!"
[Chihaya leads Mizuho toward radiology.]
[In the radiology office, Serika sits at her desk in her white coat, checking an imaging report. A soft knock comes at the door.]
"Come in."
[Chihaya opens the door, with Mizuho following her.]
"Good morning, Dr. Misono!"
"Good morning!"
[Serika looks up and pauses briefly at the sight of them.]
"...Ms. Kuramoto. Ms. Shite."
"Are we interrupting your work?"
"I've just finished a report. Come in."
[Serika puts the papers away and stands to face them.]
"Today's your discharge day, isn't it?"
"That's right! We're back in our own clothes!"
"We've just finished the paperwork. We wanted to have a word with you."
"Sit down. You're both still recovering."
"...Why do doctors always start by saying the same thing as Nurse Asakura?"
"Because you were just trying to carry my luggage!"
"...You were trying to carry luggage?"
"Oh, let's not talk about that!"
[Mizuho obediently sits down. Chihaya takes the seat beside her.]
"Dr. Misono, I already thanked you after my third operation, but... I wanted to say it again today."
"Hm?"
"After two operations, I kept thinking I was just unlucky. Why couldn't they ever get rid of that thing completely?"

"Dr. Sakaguchi and Dr. Mizuki confirmed and treated it based on what they found during surgery."
"I know. But if you hadn't found that clue, they might not have looked there, right?"
[Serika lowers her gaze slightly.]
"...There were changes on the scans that hadn't been connected before."
"So, thank you."
"And... that day on the operating table, I was still terrified even after the anesthesia."
"You'd only come to observe, but you ended up staying and talking to me for so long."
"You were the one who called me back."
"Yes! I'm so glad I did."
[Chihaya smiles.]
"I still remember you telling me our schools used to hold events together."
"...You actually remember that."
"Of course! And that you were in the light music club and played bass at the school festival!"
"Wait, Dr. Misono, you really played bass?"
"...That was in high school."
"What a surprise! I thought you'd always been this serious."
"I didn't spend every day in high school holding a scalpel."
"Pfft!"
"Hahaha! Fair point!"
[The corners of Serika's mouth move slightly.]
"I'd like to hear more about those days sometime."
"...If there's an opportunity."
[Mizuho waits until they finish, then sits a little straighter.]
"Dr. Misono, I have something to say too."
"Yes. Go ahead."
"When I was admitted, I thought surgery was nothing to be scared of. I even bragged to Chihaya that I'd definitely do better than her."
"You really did say that!"
"Let me finish!"
"But once I was actually on the operating table and saw you coming over with that marking pen... I suddenly couldn't smile anymore."
"...You were very nervous that day."
"I was! And I'd chosen to do it without anesthesia!"
"I still don't even know what to say about that."
"Neither do I... But, Dr. Misono..."

"You were stern that day, but whenever I was about to reach my limit, you told me how to breathe. What you taught me really helped."
"You kept trying to follow the rhythm, and you cooperated well with the operation."
"...But I was screaming so loudly."

"The whole operating room probably heard you."
"Chihaya!!"
"Crying out and cooperating aren't mutually exclusive."

"..."

"There it is again! Doctor-X!"
"That's the highest praise I can give!"

"...Don't mistake me for someone from a TV show."
"But it really made me happy."
"So, Dr. Misono. Thank you for that day."

"...The operation went smoothly because everyone worked together."
"Yeah. I'll go thank Dr. Sakaguchi too."
"But right now, we came especially to thank you."

"Huh? We still need an examination?"

"Y-yes, of course!"
