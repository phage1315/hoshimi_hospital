The door to the surgical department's women's changing room closed softly. Miyuki Asakura hung her white nurse's uniform in a locker and placed a set of light green scrubs on the bench.
“Phew... It's finally my turn to assist with a major operation today.”
“Nervous?”
“A little. Have you assisted with operations this long before?”
“Hmm? Of course.”
“Thank goodness. I'll follow your lead today!”
“Hehe. Remember that. Say it again when you see the instrument table.”
“Huh? Why?”
“Because then I'll ask you to concentrate on your own work first.”
Asakura put away her watch and accessories, deftly arranging her belongings in the locker. The younger nurse held a pair of scrub trousers, still studying the ties at the waist.
“Oh, these trousers tie shut. I thought they'd have a zipper like our ward uniforms.”
“You've come from the ward. It's normal to find things unfamiliar on your first day.”
Asakura put her personal clothes, including her underwear, into the locker, changed straight into the scrub trousers and top, then smoothed her sleeves.
“...Huh?”
“What is it?”
“Um... Do we have to change everything underneath too?”
“That's how we've always dressed for operations in our surgical department.”
“I thought I could just put the scrubs on over my own underwear...”
“Understandable. Nobody on the ward specifically teaches you how to change for the operating room.”
“Then... why? Does everyone dress like that?”
“At least here, we try not to wear our own undergarments during long operations. It's a nuisance if they get wet.”
“Wet? You mean from sweat?”
“That too. Irrigation fluid, blood... If you're unlucky, even your scrubs won't keep it all out.”
“Huh?! B-blood can get on our underwear?!”
The younger nurse instinctively glanced down at her clothes. Watching her suddenly stiffen, Asakura's lips curled slightly upward.
“Hehe, what's so strange? We're performing surgery, not playing house.”
“I know it's surgery! But I thought... well...”
“You thought all the blood would stay obediently inside the patient and disappear once we were done?”
“Please! Don't make it sound so frightening on purpose!”
“But I'm telling the truth.”
Asakura blinked, her smile so sweet it left little room for complaint.
“If those cute pink panties of yours get a female patient's blood on them, won't that be awkward when you get home?”
“H-how did you even see the color?!”
“I happened to catch a glimpse when you took off your ward uniform just now. I'm only worrying about them.”
“You're just making fun of me...”
“I'm not. Think about it. Hospital clothes are easy to get washed. Ruining underwear you like isn't worth it.”
“...All right. I'll go change.”
“Don't worry. Most of the time, proper protection keeps everything fine. It's just best to consider what might happen during a long operation before going in.”
“Why didn't you say the reassuring part first...?”
“Your expression was so amusing that I got a little carried away.”
The younger nurse put her undergarments in the locker and dressed properly in her scrub trousers and top. She took a few steps, stopped, and discreetly tugged at the fabric of her trousers.
“What is it? Found something else this time?”
“The trousers... They rub a little when I walk, directly against my skin.”
“Mm.”
“The top too. Without a bra, the fabric against my chest feels so rough...”
“I thought I was being too sensitive. Does it really not bother you at all?”
“The first time I wore this fabric, I thought it was stiff too.”
“Huh? You did too?”
“Of course. I'm not made out of scrub fabric.”
“I thought once you got used to it, you wouldn't even notice those things anymore.”
“You forget when you're busy. Especially when the doctor suddenly holds out a hand for an instrument. Who has time to think about their trousers chafing then?”
“That sounds a little frightening again...”
“But this batch really is a bit stiff. Let me see.”
Asakura pinched her own sleeve and compared its fabric with the younger nurse's top, then checked inside the collar.
“Mm, these must be from the new delivery. Older ones that have been washed are actually softer.”
“So it's not just me being strange?”
“Of course not. Uncomfortable clothes are uncomfortable. Putting up with them won't make them softer.”
“There are some spare sets that have softened in the wash in the next locker. I'll get you one. It should be your size.”
“Am I allowed to change?”
“Why wouldn't you, if you can? You'll be standing for hours today. You're not here for an endurance contest.”
Asakura took a folded set of scrubs from the spare locker and handed it over, then folded the stiff new set and put it aside to be checked.
“I'll mention it to the director later. At least we can get more comfortable fabric next time.”
“You take care of things like that too?”
“If we have to wear something uncomfortable all day, of course I do. Patients aren't the only people we need to look after.”
“...And you were being so mean just a moment ago.”
“Hmm? What happened a moment ago?”
“Are you really going to pretend you don't know?!”
The younger nurse finally laughed. They each picked up a surgical cap and put it on. Asakura straightened the collar of the spare scrub top for her, then checked the cap's rim, which was not quite tucked into place yet.
“Come to think of it, don't you always say you haven't much experience here at Hoshimi?”
“That's right.”
“But you even know which batch of scrubs has bad fabric. And when you mentioned long operations, you sounded like you'd done lots of them.”
“I said I was inexperienced at Hoshimi, not that I learned to be a nurse today.”
“...Oh.”
“And tuck that side of your cap in properly. Keep your ears and the ends of your hair inside.”
“Yes! I will!”
Asakura put on her mask and opened the door to the surgical corridor. The younger nurse hurried after her. Her face was still nervous, but her steps were much steadier than when she had entered the changing room.
“Um... What should I do if I really get nervous in there?”
“First, watch what I'm doing. Ask if you don't understand. Say so if you're unsure. That's much more useful than pretending to be calm.”
“Understood.”
“Good. Then let's go see whether we really are playing house today.”
“Honestly—!”
Their voices gradually faded down the surgical corridor.
